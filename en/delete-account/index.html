Delete my account and my data

App concerned: Bingo Last Breath, published by Noverane.

From the app

  1. on the home screen, tap the menu at the top right;
  2. choose Delete my account;
  3. confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

What is erased

  • your identifier, your nickname, and the linked email address or Google identity;
  • your picks and your joker in all your groups;
  • the groups you created, with all their members' picks and the non-famous people entries created in them;
  • your presence in other players' friends lists.

Before deleting a group you created, warn its members: it disappears for them too.

What remains

Non-famous people entries you created in another player's group stay in that group, no longer linked to you in any way; its creator or the person concerned can ask for them to be deleted. Public figure entries, copied from Wikidata, remain available to other players. The server's technical logs delete themselves within a few days.

If you have already uninstalled the app

If your account was protected by an email address or Google, reinstall the app, choose I already have an account, sign in, then delete it from the menu. You can also write to us from that email address.

If it stayed anonymous, the only proof of ownership was a token kept in the app. Write to novera.app.contact@gmail.com giving your nickname and the name or code of one of your groups: that is enough to find the account, which will be deleted within a month. You will not be asked for any proof of identity.

Otherwise, an account inactive for three years is deleted automatically anyway.

Deleting only part of it

  • A pick: remove it in the group, as long as the season has not started.
  • A group: Leave the group erases your picks in that group; its creator can delete it entirely.
  • A non-famous person entry: write to us with the group's name or code.
