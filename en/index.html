Bingo Last Breath

The app's reference texts. Published by Noverane. English translations; the French versions prevail in case of discrepancy.

Bingo Last Breath is a prediction game between friends about public figures' longevity. It is free, with no stakes, no purchases and no advertising. These pages say what the app does with your data and the rules of the game between us.
